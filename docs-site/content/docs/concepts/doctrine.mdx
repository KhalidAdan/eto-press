title: Constitution and doctrine
description: What every paper inherits, what an engine believes, and what is yours alone.
---

Three layers of belief stack up in every edition, and knowing which layer a
rule lives in tells you who can change it.

## The constitution — the platform's, inherited by every paper

These hold on every engine, in every tier, and no engine can opt out of them,
because they are the shape of what an engine is given rather than promises it
makes:

- **The brief ends.** Finite by construction: no infinite scroll, no related
  stories, no recommendation trained on what kept you reading.
- **The front door only.** Feeds, APIs, ordinary pages served to ordinary
  readers. A source that must be tricked into being read gets dropped.
- **The archive is fixed.** Corrections run forward, dated, pointing back.
  Nothing already printed is rewritten.
- **Readers are untracked.** No analytics, no pixels, no click-wrapping —
  on the site, in the mail, anywhere.
- **It runs on your metal.** No account, no service, no remote switch.
  Nobody can change your paper's mind by shipping an update.

## The doctrine — an engine's beliefs

Each engine states its principles, and every paper on that engine inherits
them as the editorial half of its own North Star. The
[eto engine's six](/docs/concepts/engines#the-eto-engine) — one story many
mouths, the disagreement is the story, nothing unattributed ships, the model
composites without commenting, incomplete beats wrong, the masthead is yours.
The [desk engine's three](/docs/concepts/engines#the-desk-engine) — the
editor writes, the desk is the whole corpus, silence over filler.

Disagreeing with a doctrine is what forking an engine is for. Disagreeing
with a stylesheet is not — that is what [skins](/docs/concepts/skins) are for.

## The masthead — yours alone

Name, motto, accent, sources, domain, mail identity. The press enforces the
balance you configured and no other; it will tell you plainly when your
sources collapse onto one side of a story, and it will never quietly correct
you. Point it at nine outlets that agree and it will faithfully produce a
very confident newspaper about one half of the world — a possibility the
press measures, and you own.

## What eto is not

- **Not a feed.** It has an end. That is the whole design.
- **Not a spectrum chart.** Showing you that coverage is fractured is a
  diagnosis; eto is the treatment — one readable account, sources underneath.
- **Not a fact-checker.** It does not rule on who is telling the truth. It
  tells you what each said and exactly where they part company, and hands
  the judgment back to you, which is where it belongs.
- **Not neutral by magic.** It is neutral by construction, and only as
  neutral as the file you wrote.
