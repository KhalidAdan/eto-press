---
title: The pipeline
description: Twelve stages from raw feeds to a verified edition, resumable at every step.
---

Every morning is the same walk. Two principles shape all of it:

1. **The database is the journal.** Every expensive unit of work — a feed
   fetch, a model verdict, a composite draft — is keyed and cached in SQLite.
   A crash at minute 28 costs the unit in flight and nothing else; recovery is
   always the same command, run again. Resume is not a feature; it is the
   absence of one.
2. **The model only touches prose.** Three questions are ever put to a
   model — is this the same event, which story belongs below the fold, and
   write the composite — and the stages put them to the platform's inference
   service, never to a model directly. Every other stage is deterministic
   code, and the model's output is always caged by deterministic
   verification.

## The stages

<Steps>
  <Step title="Preflight">
    Validate the masthead, run migrations, and — if the engine declares any
    models — ask the inference provider to pin its models: each one with the
    strongest identity the provider can promise (Ollama: a content digest),
    checked against `models.lock.json`. A drifted digest stops the press —
    an `ollama pull` must never silently change your paper's mind.
    Configuration problems fail loudly here, before any work.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fetch feeds">
    Every feed of every source, through the front door, with an honest
    user-agent. Every attempt is recorded — success or not — which is where
    your source-health history comes from. A failed feed never kills the run;
    the outlet is simply absent and the report says so.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Normalize and classify">
    Parse RSS and Atom (hostile encodings assumed), then classify each item —
    news, opinion, video, podcast, liveblog, digest. Only `news` items may
    participate in event matching: opinion and format items act as glue
    between unrelated clusters. An entry that arrives with its full text in
    the feed is journaled as the account of record right here; the article
    fetch later skips it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Window and prefilter">
    Keep the 48-hour window, then cut candidate pairs with plain code — two
    items must share capitalized tokens before any model sees them. This is
    a ~97% cut before a single model call. A funnel wildly off profile
    aborts the run: cheap sanity beats expensive garbage.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Judge pairs">
    One question per pair: same news event, yes or no. Verdicts are cached
    by pair, the provider's model identity, and the hash of its question —
    rerunning skips every judged pair; changing the model or the question
    re-judges automatically. Each verdict also carries a confidence, which a
    text model leaves empty. An answer that will not parse is asked once
    more, then recorded as an abstention. A unanimous stream of fresh
    verdicts trips an abort: one-sided answers mean a broken prompt, not a
    quiet news day.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Cluster, gate, dedupe">
    Union-find over the yes-edges, then a density gate that shears the
    welded blobs transitive chaining creates — no model is asked anything
    here. A blob still under the density floor after shearing is set aside
    and reported, never printed. Then clusters mostly published in an earlier
    edition are set aside — yesterday's front page does not reprint itself.
    A story that returns with mostly new reporting runs again as a
    development.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select">
    Deterministic eligibility: two or more distinct outlets, ranked by
    breadth of outlets and sides, capped — the brief ends by design. A
    one-sided story runs, carrying its measurement in print: *no source
    labeled lean-right covered this story.*
  </Step>
  <Step title="Nominate below the fold">
    One model pick from outside the front page: the story whose consequence
    most exceeds its coverage. Its reason is printed verbatim for you to
    judge, it can never displace a main story, and `below_the_fold = false`
    kills the feature entirely.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fetch the accounts">
    The full article behind every selected item — front door, politeness
    delay. An account that cannot be fetched drops out of the composite: the
    sources line names only accounts actually read. A story below two
    fetched accounts is dropped and reported.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Composite">
    The compositor is asked for the four-part story — headline, body,
    where-the-accounts-differ, sources — from at most six of the fetched
    accounts, one per outlet first, longest wins. No adjective it was not
    given, no motive, no forecast, ~350 words, it ends. The printed sources
    line is arithmetic: exactly the outlets whose accounts were in the ask.
    Whatever the model wrote under SOURCES is discarded.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify">
    The cage. Deterministic checks against the fetched text, no model. A
    violation is any of: a quoted fragment not found verbatim in an account;
    a sources line naming fewer than two outlets, or an outlet whose account
    was not fetched; prose that says "account 3" instead of naming an outlet;
    a story over the 420-word hard cap. Violations go back to the compositor
    once as editor's notes; a story that still fails drops whole, with the
    reason printed. Advisories — a named entity found in no account, a story
    over the 350-word target, an outlet fetched but not listed — print in the
    run report and never drop a story. A gap over a guess, every time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Render, archive, report">
    Surviving stories become the edition — markdown in the archive (written
    once, atomically, never overwritten), then the published-edition store
    in the journal, which the site, the email, and the RSS item are rendered
    from. The run report prints the whole morning as data: feeds read and
    failed, the funnel, blobs set aside, every drop and its named reason,
    source-health trends.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## When something fails

Every failure is a named error with structured fields, and one rule decides
its blast radius: **configuration and distribution problems stop the press
loudly; unit-of-work problems degrade the brief quietly and honestly.** A
missing outlet is a smaller brief. A broken prompt is a stopped press. The
full catalog is in [the error reference](/docs/reference/errors).
