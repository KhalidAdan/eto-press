# The platform and the engine

The press is two things, and generation 2 makes the boundary real:

**The platform** is the machinery of a personal periodical. Polite front-door
feed reading, the resumable journal, the append-only archive with corrections,
rendering to site and inbox and RSS, the subscribe flow, the schedule, the
mail. None of it knows what news is. Its guarantees are the **constitution**,
and every paper on the platform inherits them: the brief ends, the front door
only, the archive is fixed, your readers are untracked, it runs on your metal.

**An engine** is the editorial machinery that decides what a story *is* and
how it must be told. The eto engine's prefilter, judge, clustering gates,
compositor, and verification cage are one answer. A desk engine that simply
prints what the editor wrote is another. An engine's beliefs are its
**doctrine** — stated, printable, inherited by every paper on that engine.

They meet exactly once per morning, at one joint:

```text
platform:  schedule → preflight → ┐
                                  ├─ engine.edition(day) ─┐
                                  ┆   the whole middle    ┆
platform:  corrections ← render ← ┘ ← Edition | NoEdition ┘
           archive → report → mail
```

The engine is handed the morning — the date and the masthead, nothing else —
and hands back either an **edition** or **NoEdition**: true silence, no file,
no mail. A quiet page and silence are different editorial statements, and the
engine chooses which its doctrine prints.

## What an engine is never handed

The filesystem, the raw network, the archive, the mail, or your readers. An
engine reads the world through the platform's front door, reads the editor
through [the desk](/docs/concepts/engines#the-desk-engine), and remembers
through the journal. The constitution is not a promise the engine makes — it
is the shape of what the engine is given.

## Four ownership surfaces

| Surface | Owner | Contents |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Constitution | the platform | local-first, front-door reading, fixed archive, the brief ends, no tracking |
| Doctrine | an engine | the editorial principles and the anatomy of an edition they guarantee |
| Masthead | a paper | name, motto, accent, sources, domain, mail identity |
| Skin | a paper | the stylesheet — full editorial CSS rights over the engine's structure |
