---
title: The verification cage
description: The model writes; deterministic code decides what ships.
---

Two stages of the eto pipeline call a model. Everything the model produces
passes through deterministic verification before a reader sees it. This
page is why the cage looks the way it does — most of it was paid for in
live incidents and controlled experiments, kept in `docs/experiments/`.

## What the cage checks

Against the fetched account texts — never against the model's memory:

- **Every direct quote appears verbatim** in some fetched account.
- **Every named entity appears** in some fetched account.
- **Evaluative adjectives in the paper's own voice** that appear in no
  account are violations — the model may carry an outlet's
  characterization only as that outlet's.
- **The sources line names exactly** the accounts fetched and used.
- **The word budget holds** (350 target, 420 hard cap), the four parts are
  present, and nothing follows the end.

Violations become editor-style notes sent back to the compositor for
**one** revision pass — experiment 001 converged in one, so one is the
budget. Still failing: cut the offending claims if the story survives their
loss, else drop the story and print the named reason. A gap over a guess,
every time.

## Why these checks, specifically

Experiment 001's finding: the compositor's only recurring failure class was
**attribution laundering** — a source's characterization drifting into the
paper's own voice — and every instance was mechanically detectable. The
cage is that finding industrialized. The judge's failure classes
(experiment 002: transitive chaining welding unrelated stories; position
bias in candidate lists, experiment 003) got their own gates upstream: the
cluster density gate, the shuffled nomination pool, the funnel and verdict
tripwires.

## The tripwires

Statistical self-checks that abort a morning early when a stage's output
*distribution* says misconfiguration, even though every individual call
succeeded:

- `FunnelAnomalous` — hundreds of news items but zero candidate pairs (or
  20× the profile). A broken prefilter, caught before model time.
- `VerdictsSuspicious` — the first hundred verdicts unanimous. A broken
  prompt teaches nothing for twenty more minutes; stop now.

Experiment 002 paid for this lesson: its zero-matches run failed silently,
politely, and completely.

## Degrade quietly, stop loudly

The dividing line runs through the whole codebase: a fetch that fails
shrinks the brief and says so; a compositor that stops answering stops the
press (`PressStalled`) rather than absorbing timeouts as story drops. The
2026-08-02 incident — eight straight timeouts printed a zero-story edition
— is why that error exists and why it is fatal.

## Advisories

Checks worth recording but not enforcing (a story slightly over the soft
word target, an account fetched but unnamed) print in the run report as
advisories. They are the cage's gray zone made visible — candidates for
promotion to real checks when a pattern emerges.
