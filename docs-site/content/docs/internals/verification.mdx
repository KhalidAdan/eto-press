---
title: The verification cage
description: The model writes; deterministic code decides what ships.
---

The eto pipeline puts three questions to the inference service — same
event, the below-the-fold nomination, the composite — and everything that
comes back as prose passes through deterministic verification before a
reader sees it. This page is why the cage looks the way it does — most of
it was paid for in live incidents and controlled experiments, kept in
`docs/experiments/`.

## What the cage checks

`packages/engine-eto/src/verify.ts`, string work only, against the fetched
account texts — never against the model's memory. Four checks are
violations:

- **Every quoted fragment appears verbatim** in some fetched account.
- **The sources line names two or more outlets**, every one of them an
  outlet whose account was fetched. The line itself is computed by stage 8
  from the accounts that were in the ask — the model's own SOURCES text is
  discarded — so this check guards the arithmetic, not the model.
- **Prose never says "account 3".** A numbered reference is prompt
  scaffolding the reader cannot see (the 2026-08-01 Ceuta story).
- **The hard word cap holds** — 420 words across headline, body, and differ.

Three are advisories — recorded, printed in the run report, never a reason
to drop:

- A named entity in the paper's voice that appears in no account.
  Advisory until the false-positive rate is measured.
- The story is over the 350-word target but under the cap.
- An outlet was fetched but is not in the sources line.

There is no adjective check. The compositor's standing orders forbid any
adjective it was not given; nothing verifies that, and this page does not
pretend otherwise.

The four-part shape is not the cage's business. A completion that does not
parse into headline, body, differ, and sources is null at the boundary;
stage 8 asks once more and then drops the story as `DraftMalformed`, before
the cage ever sees it.

Violations become editor-style notes sent back to the compositor for
**one** revision pass — experiment 001 converged in one, so one is the
budget. Still failing, the story drops whole (`BriefUnverifiable`,
story-scoped) and the report prints the named reason. Claims are never cut
from a story; a gap over a guess, every time.

## Why these checks, specifically

Experiment 001's finding: the compositor's only recurring failure class was
**attribution laundering** — a source's characterization drifting into the
paper's own voice — and every instance was mechanically detectable. The
cage is that finding industrialized. The judge's failure classes
(experiment 002: transitive chaining welding unrelated stories; position
bias in candidate lists, experiment 003) got their own gates upstream: the
cluster density gate, the shuffled nomination pool, the funnel and verdict
tripwires.

## The tripwires

Statistical self-checks that abort a morning early when a stage's output
*distribution* says misconfiguration, even though every individual call
succeeded:

- `FunnelAnomalous` — more than 20 news items but zero candidate pairs, or
  candidate pairs exceeding 20× the news items. A broken prefilter, caught
  before model time.
- `VerdictsSuspicious` — the first hundred *fresh* verdicts (cached ones
  do not count) unanimous. A broken prompt teaches nothing for twenty more
  minutes; stop now.

Experiment 002 paid for this lesson: its zero-matches run failed silently,
politely, and completely.

## Degrade quietly, stop loudly

The dividing line runs through the whole codebase: a fetch that fails
shrinks the brief and says so; a compositor that stops answering stops the
press (`PressStalled`) rather than absorbing timeouts as story drops. The
2026-08-02 incident — eight straight timeouts printed a zero-story edition
— is why that error exists and why it is fatal.

## Advisories

Checks worth recording but not enforcing (an entity found in no account, a
story over the soft word target, an account fetched but unnamed) print in
the run report as advisories and are journaled in `verifications` beside
the violations. They are the cage's gray zone made visible — candidates for
promotion to real checks when a pattern emerges.
