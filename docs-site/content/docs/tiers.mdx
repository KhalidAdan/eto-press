---
title: The tier ladder
description: Publicness is a ladder, and you only pay for the rung you stand on.
---


Most newsletter tooling charges every user the full public-publishing price —
cloud accounts, DNS, mail providers — even to read their own words. eto's
installation cost scales with how public your paper is, and the private tier
is the one everything else is measured against.

## Tier 1 — a paper for yourself

Ollama, Node, `sources.toml`. Output is `site/` opened locally or on your LAN.
Zero accounts, zero secrets, working fifteen minutes after install. This tier
is the constitution's "it runs on your metal" made literal.

## Tier 2 — a paper on the web

Add a static host. Publishing is an adapter named in configuration — Cloudflare
Pages, Netlify, rsync, or a plain folder — not a workflow you maintain. Your
archive is static HTML; anything that can serve files can serve your paper.

## Tier 3 — a paper with a mailing list

Only here does a mail provider enter. `eto mail setup ses` walks the
provisioning — domain identity, DKIM, the contact list, suppression — and the
subscribe flow deploys only for this tier. The press's send guards are
idempotent: a retried morning never mails twice.

## Climbing is optional

A Tier 1 paper is not a demo. It is the complete product: the same pipeline,
the same verification, the same archive. The ladder exists so that the cost of
an audience is paid only by papers that want one.
