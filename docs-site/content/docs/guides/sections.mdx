<Callout>
  Sections ship with **generation 3**. A paper that declares none is a
  paper with one section — the brief, printed by `[engine] use` from
  `sources.toml` — and prints exactly as it did.
</Callout>

A paper is several desks. Each desk is one engine reading one source file,
and the press binds them into one dated edition: the first section in full
on the front page, every other desk compressed to one row per item under
it, each desk at its own address one click deeper. The archive holds every
morning whole.

## Declare the desks

In `eto.toml`, one `[[section]]` per desk, in the order they print:

```toml
[[section]]
name = "Current events brief"
slug = "brief"
engine = "eto"
masthead = "sources.toml"      # the file the brief has always read

[[section]]
name = "Business"
slug = "business"
engine = "ledger"              # boards, columns, decked links

[[section]]
name = "Sports"
slug = "sports"
engine = "ledger"

[[section]]
name = "Blogs"
slug = "blogs"
engine = "shelf"               # the writers you follow, one line each
```

Each section's source file defaults to `sections/<slug>.toml` and has
[the shape `sources.toml` has](/docs/reference/sources-file). What `side`
means in it is the engine's to say: a stance on the eto engine, a shelf on
the shelf engine, a board or a shelf on the ledger.

## Write the source files

**The brief** keeps `sources.toml`: outlets, where they stand, the
`[seed]` claim.

**Business** on the ledger engine mixes two kinds of source. A `door` is a
data endpoint carrying a figure; a feed is an outlet whose new posts print
as decked rows:

```toml
[[source]]
name = "USD/CAD"
side = "Markets"
kind = "door"
feeds = ["https://api.frankfurter.app/latest?from=USD&to=CAD#rates.CAD"]

[[source]]
name = "BBC Business"
side = "Business news"
feeds = ["https://feeds.bbci.co.uk/news/business/rss.xml"]
```

**Sports** is the same shape: the leagues' scoreboards as doors (a
keyless scoreboard served to a league's own readers is a front door),
the writers you trust as feeds, and your own signed columns on the desk.

**Blogs** on the shelf engine is writers only, `side` naming the shelf:

```toml
[[source]]
name = "Daring Fireball"
side = "Tech"
feeds = ["https://daringfireball.net/feeds/main"]
```

One rule the press enforces at preflight: **a feed URL belongs to one
section only.** Two desks may read the same outlet through different
feeds; they may not share one.

## The desk is per section

Once sections are declared, editor-written copy lives under the section:
`desk/sports/2026-09-26-on-the-bench.md` prints in Sports, bylined, and
never in Blogs. The single-section paper keeps `desk/`.

## What prints

- **The front page**: the first section in full, then "In today's paper"
  — every other desk as one row per item (kicker, headline, one-line deck,
  source), its board's figures above the rows. The ears under the
  nameplate carry the desks' counts.
- **Section pages** at `/YYYY-MM-DD/<slug>/`, with "Section 2 of 4" and
  the neighbouring desks.
- **The whole paper** at `/YYYY-MM-DD.html`, every desk labeled.
- **The archive** as a calendar of mornings: each day its lead headline
  and how many desks printed.
- **The email**: the first section in full, then one card for another
  desk that printed this morning (the run id picks it, so a retry mails
  the same card), and two short lines saying there is more on the site.
- **Feeds**: `/feed.xml` keeps carrying the first section; `/<slug>/feed.xml`
  for every desk.

## When a desk is quiet

A section whose engine has nothing to print — no new posts, nothing
moved, an empty desk — is **absent** that morning: a line on the page
("Not printed this morning: Sports — no new columns and no new league
links") and a `WARNING` in the report, for you to look into. The other
desks print. A morning where every desk is silent prints nothing at all.

## Models

Preflight pins the union of the desks' models. A paper of desk, digest,
letter, sports and wrap sections never wakes Ollama. The shelf and the
ledger declare the judge model for [the deck](/docs/concepts/engines#the-shelf-engine)
and ask it only for posts whose feeds carried no summary of their own.

## The morning delivery switch

`email_edition` moves to `eto.toml`, under `[mail]`, because it is the
paper's, not a section's. Undeclared there, the flag in the first section's
source file decides, where generation 2 kept it.

`eto doctor` checks every section's source file, the one-feed-one-section
rule, and each desk directory.
