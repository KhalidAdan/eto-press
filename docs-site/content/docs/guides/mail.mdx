A paper with readers sends one email a day. It ends, like the brief ends:
no tracking pixels, no click wrapping, an unsubscribe link in every footer
that actually works.

## Setting up SES

Amazon SES is the recommended provider for a real mailing list — roughly the
cost of a coffee per year at household scale. The provisioning is one command
and two DNS records:

```bash
eto mail setup ses
```

This creates the domain identity, DKIM records, the contact list, the
suppression handling, and the configuration set, then wires the secrets. Flip
`email_edition = true` in your masthead when the domain verifies.

## The guards

Sends are idempotent — a retried morning never mails twice. Suppressed
addresses are swept before every send. Quota pacing respects your provider's
limits. On a morning with no edition, nothing leaves.

## The subscribe flow

Tier 3 deploys a small pair of edge functions: a form endpoint with a
honeypot, and a confirm link. Readers opt in twice; your list is yours,
exportable, and never enriched with anything you did not ask for.
