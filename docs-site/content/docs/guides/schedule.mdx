---
title: The paperboy
description: The paper prints itself before you wake.
---


`eto press` is the whole morning as one verb: print the edition, render the
site, export the journal, send the email, take the backups. `eto schedule`
installs it into your operating system's scheduler — Task Scheduler on
Windows, launchd on macOS, cron or systemd on Linux — so it runs before you
wake.

## Retries are safe

Every stage is idempotent by construction. If today's edition already exists,
the print is skipped but the tail still runs — render, export, email, backups
— so an hourly retry after a partial morning *finishes* the morning instead of
failing at a guard.

## The one caution

The pipeline wants your GPU to itself. If other model servers (a speech
model, a second LLM) share the card at press time, the compositor can starve
and the press will stop loudly rather than print a hollow edition. `eto
doctor` names this check; scheduling around your other workloads is the cure.
