## Is this a fact-checker?

No. eto does not rule on who is telling the truth. It tells you what each
outlet said and exactly where they part company, and hands the judgment back
to you, which is where it belongs.

## Why local models? Wouldn't GPT-whatever write better stories?

Probably — and it could also change its mind by shipping an update, which is
precisely the property a paper cannot have. The guarantee is not that eto is
trustworthy; it is that eto *cannot become untrustworthy without your hands
on it*. Local, digest-pinned models are that guarantee made mechanical. The
compositor's job is merge, compress, attribute — a caged 8B model does that
verifiably; eloquence was never the point.

## Can I point it at my favorite outlets and nothing else?

Yes, and the press will tell you what you built: point it at nine outlets
that agree and it will faithfully produce a very confident newspaper about
one half of the world, with the balance notes to prove it. Neutral by
construction means only as neutral as the file you wrote.

## What does it cost to run?

Tier 1: electricity. The models run on your GPU; the feeds are free; there
are no accounts. Tier 2 adds a static host (usually free). Tier 3 adds mail
at roughly the cost of a coffee per year at household scale.

## How is this different from an RSS reader?

A reader hands you ninety items; eto hands you eight stories, each one event
told through outlets that disagree, differences named, sources linked, and
then it ends. It is the difference between a firehose and a paper.

## Can my paper have readers?

That is the oldest and best shape of the press: one editor, a masthead they
own, and whoever cares to read it. An edition with readers says whose
masthead it is, on the page and in the mail, so any reader can find the
editorial line, question it, and — because the press is free — go run their
own. That is not a warning label; it is the invitation.

## What about a paper that's just my own writing?

The desk engine: markdown in, editions out, no models, no feeds — the same
archive, mail, and no-tracking constitution. See
[Run a desk paper](/docs/guides/desk-paper). (Generation 2.)

## Why doesn't it use my browser / log in to paywalled sites?

The front door only. eto does not disguise itself, does not pretend to be a
browser it is not, does not pick a lock a publisher has closed. Politeness
is not only ethics — it is why the press runs untouched on your hardware
for years while a cleverer thing would have broken by spring.

## Is my reading tracked? Are my readers?

No, and no. No analytics on the site, no pixels in the mail, no
click-wrapping, nothing trained on what kept anyone reading. In any tier.

## What's the license?

The published packages currently carry AGPL-3.0 — the press is free, and a
hosted fork owes its readers its source. The final licensing decision is
still on the editor's desk, and will be settled before the generation-2 line
publishes; whatever it lands on, the invitation to run your own paper stays
structural.
