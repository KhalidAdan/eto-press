---

eto takes a single news event, gathers the accounts of it published by outlets
that disagree, and writes one piece of prose that holds all of them. It names
every source it used. Then it stops.

It runs on your machine — your model, your electricity, your source list. There
is no account, no service, and no remote switch. From the moment you install it,
you are not its reader. **You are its editor.**

## What a story looks like

Every story has four parts: what happened, where the accounts part company, who
said so, and an end.

```text
IDF claims Hamas headquartered under Al-Shifa

  Israeli troops entered Al-Shifa Hospital in Gaza after
  encountering fighters at the gate. The IDF had surrounded
  the hospital, saying Hamas had established a command post
  in tunnels beneath it. Staff were told in advance.

  Where the accounts differ
  Hamas denies the tunnel claim outright. The Guardian notes
  the search results were unreleased at press time; FOX
  reports White House officials corroborating the Israeli
  account. No source reports having seen the tunnels.

  Sources  The Guardian · Reuters · FOX
```

The third part is old — every honest outlet cites. The fourth is rare. The
second is the one that does not exist anywhere else, and it is the reason this
exists.

## What it will never do

No infinite scroll. No recommendations. No tracking — not of you, and not of
your readers. The brief ends today the way it ended yesterday, and you leave.
A product that cannot be finished is not informing you; it is holding you.
